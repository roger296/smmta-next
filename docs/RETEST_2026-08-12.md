# Retest script — repeating the 12 August 2026 venue session

A numbered manual script that **mirrors the original test**: the same two
devices, the same delivery, the same three screens, in the same order. The
point is a like-for-like comparison, not a fresh explore — so please work
through it as written, and note anything that differs rather than working
around it.

Every step names the defect it is re-testing (`A-1` … `F-8`), so a failure can
be reported as "step 14 — C-1 is back" and go straight to the right place.
The register itself is `docs/FEEDBACK_2026-08-12.md`.

---

## Before you start (head office)

These are data, not code, and the retest is not meaningful without them.

1. **The real cake menu** imported per `docs/RECIPE_IMPORT.md`, including the
   gluten-free and vegan swaps per cake. No demo cakes should appear anywhere.
2. **The real ingredient catalogue** imported — purchase units, pack sizes,
   costs and barcodes. Work the **Needs setup** report (Admin → Needs setup)
   to zero first; anything left on it will fail step 12 or 14.
3. **Each iPad's PIN is bound to its venue.** Check Admin → Device PINs. E-1 is
   fixed in code, but the binding itself is data.
4. *(removed)* — there is no bench setting to configure. A bench and a table
   are the same thing, so the per-site ratio added on 20 Aug was dropped the
   same day. Nothing to do here.
5. **12 August data treated as suspect** — the 100 kg Birmingham booking
   reversed, and any counts taken that day recounted.

## What you need on the day

- The venue **iPad**, with Big Bakes Stock added to the home screen.
- A **laptop**, signed in to the admin site.
- A delivery matching the original: **4 × 25 kg icing sugar (SLR)** and
  **4 × 1.6 kg Skittles**. If the real delivery differs, note the actual packs
  — the pack size is the thing under test, not the product.
- The PIN for this venue.

## 1 — Entry and sign-in (E-2, E-6, B-5)

1. Turn the iPad to **portrait**. Tap the Big Bakes Stock icon **on the home
   screen** (not a Safari tab).
   - ✅ It opens the **PIN keypad**, not the email login form. *(E-2)*
   - ✅ The screen names **this venue** above the keypad. *(B-5)*
2. Rotate to **landscape**. ✅ It rotates; it does not stay locked to portrait. *(E-6)*
3. Enter the PIN and sign in.
   - ✅ You land on the **three-job venue home** — Goods In, End of Bake, Stock
     Take — not the desktop dashboard.
   - ✅ The venue name across the top is **this venue**. *(E-1)*
4. ✅ In landscape there is a **navigation rail** down the left with the three
   jobs; in portrait there is a **Menu** button in the top bar that opens the
   same list. The job you are on is marked. *(B-7)*

## 2 — Goods In (A-1, A-5, A-6, B-1…B-3, C-1…C-6, D-4, D-5, E-3, E-5)

5. Open **Goods In**.
   - ✅ The keyboard does **not** open by itself. *(B-2)*
   - ✅ The top of the screen is fully visible. *(B-1)*
6. Tap the product-code box and type the **icing sugar barcode**.
   - ✅ The top bar and the code box stay on screen with the keyboard open.
     Nothing is cut off, and you can still reach everything. *(B-1)*
   - ✅ Try scrolling the background behind the screen: nothing moves. *(B-3)*
7. Tap **+ Add**.
   - ✅ The product is found by its barcode. *(C-3)*
   - ✅ The line reads **"1 × 25 kg sack = 25 kg"** — not "= 1 g". *(C-1)*
   - ✅ The cost shows both **£30.00/sack** and **£0.0012/g** — not £0.00. *(C-4)*
8. Tap **+1 sack** three times. ✅ The line reads **4 × 25 kg sack = 100 kg**. *(C-6)*
9. Tap the **quantity number**, and on the laptop keyboard type `3`.
   - ✅ The field reads **3**, not **43** or **13**. *(D-4)*
   - ✅ Typing on a physical keyboard works at all, and **Enter** confirms. *(D-5)*
   - Set it back to **4**.
10. Add the **Skittles** the same way.
    - ✅ The line reads **1 × 1.6 kg bag = 1.6 kg**. *(C-2)*
    - Step it up to **4 bags**.
11. Type a **barcode that does not exist** (e.g. `0000000000000`) and tap Add.
    - ✅ A sheet appears saying **"Nothing found for …"**, offering a search by
      name and an option to attach the code. It does not silently do nothing. *(A-6)*
    - Close the sheet.
12. Tap the **£** button on the icing sugar line.
    - ✅ You can set a unit cost. If you are signed in as a head baker it should
      say a site manager is needed — that is the role split working, not a
      bug. *(C-5, E-4)*
13. Tap **Book in 2 lines**.
    - ✅ A confirmation appears naming **this venue, large**, and listing what
      is about to be booked. *(E-5)*
14. Tap **Confirm and book in**.
    - ✅ A **receipt** appears and **stays** on screen with the totals, plus a
      **Book another** button. The lines do not just vanish. *(A-5)*
    - ✅ An **Undo** bar appears naming the venue. *(E-3)*
15. Tap **Undo** within 90 seconds. ✅ It reverses; the bar disappears.
16. Tap **Book another** and re-book the delivery properly.

## 3 — Stock Take (D-1, D-2, D-3, A-3, A-4)

17. Open **Stock Take** and start a full count.
    - ✅ Every row shows a **real product name and stock code**, not an
      eight-character hex fragment. *(D-1)*
18. Type **`ING-ICING`** (or a real stock code) in the search box.
    - ✅ The list filters to that item. *(D-3)*
    - ✅ It filters **immediately** — no lag on a full sheet.
19. Count the icing sugar as **4000 g (4 kg)** and save the count.
    - ✅ The row reads **4000**. Not 0, not 4100. *(D-2)*
20. Tap **Save counts**. ✅ The pill at the top says **All saved**.
21. As a **site manager**, tap **Approve & true-up**. ✅ It approves.
    - As a head baker the button should not be there. *(E-4)*

## 4 — End of Bake (F-1 … F-8)

22. Open **End of Bake**. Pick a cake.
    - ✅ Only cakes **Big Bakes actually sells** are listed. *(F-4)*
    - ✅ If the cake has no gluten-free or vegan version, that bench field is
      **disabled** and says to ask head office — it does not accept a number
      that quietly does nothing. *(F-5)*
23. Enter **5 regular benches**, plus any GF/vegan benches the cake supports.
    Fill in the session id and your name, and tap **Load ingredients**.
    - ✅ Ingredients appear. If they do not, you should get a **blocking notice**
      naming the cake, the date and the venue, saying the bake cannot be
      submitted — never an empty list. *(F-6)*
    - ✅ Selecting GF or vegan benches changes the ingredient list — substitutes
      appear and the standard ones drop. *(F-5)*
24. On one ingredient, tap **−1 bench**.
    - ✅ The quantity goes **down**, and the count between the buttons goes
      from 5 / 5 to **4 / 5**. *(F-3)*
25. Tap **ENTERING: AMOUNT USED** to switch to **What's Left**.
    - ✅ Tap **+1 bench left**: the number goes **UP**. Tap **−1 bench left**:
      it goes **DOWN**. The plain + and − agree with them. *(F-1)*
26. Tap the toggle again to switch **back** to amount used.
    - ✅ The original figure is **still there**, not zeroed. *(F-2)*
27. ✅ Under each ingredient there is a **bench count** — e.g. `4 of 5 benches`
    — that tracks the quantity as you change it, so you can check it against
    the room after an interruption. *(F-7)*
28. Leave one line in **What's Left** without counting it.
    - ✅ The submit button says **"1 line not counted yet"** and is disabled.
29. Count it, then **Submit consumption**. ✅ It submits without error. *(F-8)*

## 5 — Offline and rejection (A-1, A-2, A-3, A-4)

30. Open **Goods In** and add one line. **Turn off Wi-Fi on the iPad.**
31. Book it in.
    - ✅ The pill says **Offline — 1 waiting** (or **Pending 1**). *(A-3)*
32. Tap the pill.
    - ✅ A drawer lists the unsent work, so you can see exactly what has not
      gone in. *(A-4)*
33. **Turn Wi-Fi back on** and wait.
    - ✅ It sends **by itself**, without you navigating anywhere, and the pill
      returns to **All saved**. *(A-2)*
34. Ask head office to make the next booking fail (or book to a site the device
    is not bound to).
    - ✅ You get a **red error banner** saying it was refused, the lines **stay
      on screen**, and the pill does **not** say the work is saved. *(A-1)*

## 6 — Laptop / admin (B-6, B-7)

35. On the laptop, open the admin site.
    - ✅ The **venue selector** in the header is readable — dark text on white,
      not white on white. *(B-6)*
36. Collapse the sidebar with the toggle, navigate to another page, then
    **reload**.
    - ✅ It stays collapsed, and the page you are on is still marked. *(B-7)*
    - ✅ The breadcrumb line names the section you are in.
37. Move between two pages.
    - ✅ The change is a short fade, not an abrupt jump. *(B-7)*

## Reporting

For anything that fails, please note:

- the **step number** and the **defect ID** on it;
- the **device** (iPad portrait / iPad landscape / laptop);
- what you saw, in the words you would use to describe it to a colleague;
- a photo of the screen if the wording matters.

Anything new — not on this script — is just as welcome. Put it at the end
under **New**, and it will get its own ID.
